Privacy Policy - 2048 Play

This page explains what data 2048 Play may process, in what context, and how to contact Edab Studios.

Application covered

2048 Play is an Edab Studios mobile game built around a 4 x 4 board, tile merging, and short replayable sessions.

Information that may be processed

  • Device model, operating system, and technical runtime information
  • Application usage data, including gameplay-related session information
  • Diagnostic, performance, or crash information
  • Advertising identifiers if third-party advertising services are used in the published product

Advertising and consent

2048 Play may display advertising through third-party services, including Google AdMob depending on the published product configuration.

Depending on your region, a consent form may appear so you can manage choices related to personalized advertising.

Local storage and progression

The application may store some settings or progression items locally, such as a best score, an ongoing run, or operational preferences.

This data helps maintain a consistent game experience and makes it easier to resume a session.

Security and children

We make reasonable efforts to protect information processed through the application and its technical components.

The application is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
