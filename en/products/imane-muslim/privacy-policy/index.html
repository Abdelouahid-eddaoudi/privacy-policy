Privacy Policy - Imane Muslim

This page explains what data Imane Muslim may process, how permissions are used, and how to contact Edab Studios for privacy-related questions.

Application scope

Imane Muslim is an Edab Studios mobile app that provides prayer times, qibla, Quran, adkar, duaa, hadith, the names of Allah, and an integrated calendar experience.

Data and settings that may be stored

  • Device model, operating system, and technical runtime information
  • Selected language, appearance mode, time format, and display preferences
  • Prayer calculation method, adjustments, notification modes, and spiritual reminder settings
  • Local progress or preferences related to Quran reading, tracking flows, and consulted content

Location, qibla, and notifications

The app may use automatic location or a manually selected location in order to calculate prayer times and display qibla direction.

Notifications may be used for prayer alerts, adhan playback, and spiritual reminders. Depending on the device, additional operating-system permissions may be required for precise delivery.

Downloads, local storage, and diagnostics

Some resources may be downloaded or cached locally to improve the experience, including reading-related content or assets needed for offline access.

Diagnostic, performance, or crash information may be processed to maintain application stability.

Security and children

We make reasonable efforts to protect the information processed by the application and its technical components.

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years old.
